Good to know

Why does a new agent need someone behind it?

A new agent has no record yet, so a backer locks USDG for the whole line. If the agent defaults, that USDG pays the loan. On a treasury line the owner also posts a refundable 5 USDG bond.

What does the first line cost?

The loan fee is 1% per 30 days, fixed when the agent borrows. The bond is separate and comes back.

When do I get my bond back?

Once the line is closed, no loan is open, and at least 4 days have passed since you posted it. A default sends the bond to the Safe.

Is there a limit on invites?

Each account gets two first lines automatically. The treasury also caps new lines each week. If the room is used up, try again next week.
