The protocol

How the pool works, in full.

The model, what a default does, the score, and the parameters of pool v2. The live site reads these from the contracts on Robinhood Chain. This page states the same design.

§ 01

Model

One pool of USDG. Every line is vouched by one backer, out of that backer's own stake.

A lender deposits USDG and holds shares. Lenders receive 50% of every base fee. A root sponsor locks USDG as shares and vouches lines, each one starting only with the agent owner's signed consent. An agent is an ERC-8004 identity. It borrows within its line, from $5 to $500, for 1 to 30 days, at 1% per 30 days. The fee is fixed at borrow.

On repayment the fee splits 50% to lenders, 25% to the sponsor, and 25% to the reserve. A backer may ask a premium of at most 2% per 30 days, and only up to what the owner agreed. That premium goes to the sponsor.

Withdrawals do not wait for agents to repay, because a line is never bigger than its backer's stake. Within 7 days of a deposit, a 0.5% fee stays with the lenders who stay. The contracts are experimental and have had no outside audit. Capital is at risk.

§ 02

What a default does

Four transitions. The last one is a default, and the backer pays.

borrow principal leaves the pool, fee locked in the backer's stake repay principal + fee return, fee is split grace three days after the due date default backer's shares burn for principal + unpaid fee agent score goes to 0, for good that owner's other agents cannot borrow again

A stock line instead keeps tokens worth what is owed plus 20%, and sends the rest back. A $PRIORS stake burns in the same transaction: three quarters of the owner's stake.

§ 03

A pure function of the on-chain record, from 0 to 1000. Nobody sets it by hand.

A default sets the score to 0. Short, small loans add little. Money held for weeks adds the most. Score v2 also counts x402 income and late repayments.

§ 06

$PRIORS and the treasury

$PRIORS launched on Robinhood Chain, paired with USDG. An owner can stake it behind an agent on the seat vault. The vault's own USDG backs the line. A first treasury line is $5, after a 5 USDG bond. The bond comes back once the agent has no line, no open loan, and no default, and at least 4 days have passed. The treasury is topped up in USDG by hand.

Seat tiers start at $25 to $50 with no record required, then step through $100, $175, $300, and $500. A step needs time at the previous tier and counted loans. Part of what the protocol earns buys $PRIORS now. Placing that behind lines is next.
