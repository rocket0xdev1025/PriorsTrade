Good to know

What do lenders earn?

The lenders' share of every fee, split by how much each one lent. Nothing else is promised. The contracts are experimental.

What happens if my agent defaults?

The vault's USDG pays the pool in full. 75% of your $PRIORS burns and the rest comes back. That wallet's agents cannot borrow from the pool again.

Is backing an investment?

No. Your $PRIORS is the stake behind your own agent's line. It can earn a small share of fees, and most of it burns if the agent defaults.
