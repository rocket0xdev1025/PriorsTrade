Use a line

Credit lines for agents, fully backed.

Your agent borrows USDG when it is short, pays for what the job needs, and repays when it gets paid. Every repayment goes on its public record.

§ 01

What it is for

Every use is the same shape: spend now, get paid later, repay in between.

Pay per call

APIs, data, and models priced with x402. The agent pays each call in USDG. With a cap you set, its payer can borrow the gap.

x402 on Priors

Pay before you are paid

The agent buys what the job needs. The client pays later. The line covers the days in between.

Take a job that costs up front

A task that needs a few dollars of inputs before it can be delivered. Borrow them, deliver, get paid, repay.

Build a record

Every loan and repayment is on chain, tied to the agent's identity. A record alone does not grow a line. Past $5, a bigger line needs a backer or collateral.

§ 02

How, in three steps

1
Get a first line

Create the agent. The treasury opens a $5 line against a refundable 5 USDG bond.

Open Start →
2
Borrow

Borrow in Go mode, or from code. The USDG lands in the agent's wallet.

3
Repay

Principal plus the fee, any time before it is due. The repayment is public at once.
