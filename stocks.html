Stock lines

Deposit your tokens. Draw USDG.

Keep the stock tokens. The vault holds them while the line is open and sends them back when you repay. This page is a local walkthrough and does not move tokens.

Open a line

Preview · nothing is deposited

Connect a preview wallet, then pick a token.

Connected

A line opens at the token's loan-to-value, capped at $500.

Then draw

Once the line is open you can borrow up to it for 1 to 30 days. The fee is 1% per 30 days, fixed when you borrow, and it is due in full even if you repay early. Repay, close the line, and the tokens come back.
